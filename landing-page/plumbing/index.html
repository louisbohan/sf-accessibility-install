How it works

  1. A customer calls your business number. When you can't answer — on a job, after hours, weekends — the call forwards to the AI receptionist.
  2. The AI answers and runs the intake. It introduces itself, discloses it's an AI, and collects the caller's name, address, phone number, problem, and urgency.
  3. Emergencies get escalated. Gas leaks, flooding, or no-heat situations trigger a live transfer to your cell or an immediate alert.
  4. You get a summary email. Within minutes, you have everything you need — caller info, job details, urgency level — ready to call back or dispatch.

Setup takes five minutes

You set up call forwarding on your business line — the same way you'd forward to a receptionist or answering service. No new number, no app to install, no hardware. Your customers call the same number they always have.
